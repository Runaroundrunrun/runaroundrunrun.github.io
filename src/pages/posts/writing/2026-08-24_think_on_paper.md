When I have to spend time alone with my brain, without books or a phone, I have little control over the voice in my head.  
This voice - it isn't clear enough -  has power to make me feel depressed.  

For example, I tend to feel down as I read job requirements. It seems like the voice in my head is telling me that I'm not qualified for any job.  

This is different when I write down what I think or what the voice says, writing helps me be more objective of the voice, my thoughts and feelings.  
It keeps me from ruminating, which is I'm good at doing when I'm alone with my brain.  

That's probably why I usually feel less depressed when I think on paper.

So dear myself in the future, please please think on paper, especially when you notice you're having negative thoughts, and try to stay calm.
