It seems like now is not the best time to start a blog or create a personal website.  
People visit the websites less than before (probably because of *Ehm,* the rise of AI search?)

There is little return even if you write online, especially when you want money and attention out of it.

Despite the current situation, here are the posts that inspired me to start a blog and keep writing:

- Alexey Guzey, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://guzey.com/personal/why-have-a-blog/">Why You Should Start a Blog Right Now</a>
- alexandra, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://library.xandra.cc/everyone-should-blog/">EveryoneShouldBlog.txt</a> (the library of alexandra)
- Aaron Francis, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://github.com/readme/guides/publishing-your-work">Publishing your work increases your luck</a> (github.com)
- Chuck Grimmett, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://cagrimmett.com/2022/04/26/why-blog/">Why blog?</a>
- steve yegge, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://sites.google.com/site/steveyegge2/you-should-write-blogs">you-should-write-blogs</a>
- Tom Critchlow, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://tomcritchlow.com/2022/03/08/architecture-blogging/">Building a Digital Homestead, Bit by Brick</a>
- leticia portella, <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://leportella.com/why-have-a-blog.html/">Why you should have a blog (and write in it)</a>
