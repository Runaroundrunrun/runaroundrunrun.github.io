For at least two years, I have been listening to “Scarlett” and “Room Service” by Holly Humberstone.  
I didn't check her newer songs for a while, but recently I stumbled upon her new album “Cruel World”.

I love it so much now, I wish I had listened to it sooner! 

Here's the tracklist:
1. So It Starts...
2. Make It All Better
3. To Love Somebody
4. Cruel World
5. Die Happy
6. White Noise
7. Lucy
8. Red Chevy
9. Drunk Dialling
10. Peachy
11. Blue Dream
12. Beauty Pageant

I was especially stunned by “To Love Somebody”, “Die Happy” and “Beauty Pageant” first.  
As I listen to the album, I grew to like the other songs too. Each song feels like a chapter of a big good fantasy story or a play.

I loved her performance as well!
