Their US debut EP ANIMAL was released on October 2nd, and I’ve been listening to it since then.

Here's the tracklist:
1. ANIMAL
2. WHATCHA DOIN
3. SAKURA
4. THIS DAT FYAH
5. DOWN
6. STATUS

I had already heard and loved *WHATCHA DOIN* and *SAKURA* when they became available, but I never expected that I would be addicted to the songs in the EP like now.  
I think I love this EP more than anything they had released before.

Especially I never get tired of listening to *ANIMAL*'s bridge part, the first rap part in *STATUS*, the groove of *THIS DAT FYAH* [^1]... how can they be so good?

The music videos of *ANIMAL* and *WHATCHA DOIN* are also very cool!
