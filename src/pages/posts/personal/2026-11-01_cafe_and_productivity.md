Writing, reading, thinking about how I want to alter this website... I can focus on those activities more in a cafe than doing them from home.

I think it's because there are less distractions when I'm at a cafe, for I don't bring my tablet with me and I use my phone less.
Clutters in my room also seem to bother me.

One of other possible reasons is that there's a time limit and you can't stay there forever.
When I have time at home, it feels like I can start or continue whenever I want. And because I feel that way, it's harder to start the things I want to do, I'd be like "it doesn't have to be done right now."

Or maybe, the beverage I order at a cafe usually contains caffeine and sugar, so it boosts my focus and energy.

But I don't want to be the person who needs to go to a cafe every time when there's a work that needs to be done...  
Maybe things like below might help me be more productive at home:
- Setting up a dedicated space where there is no clutter or stuff in my sight.
- Planning how much I work on the target task beforehand.
