Here are the songs from Holly Humberstone's EP *The Walls Are Way Too Thin* [^1]:
1. Haunted House
2. The Walls Are Way Too Thin
3. Please Don't Leave Just Yet
4. Thursday
5. Scarlett
6. Friendly Fire

This EP gives me different impression from her album [*Cruel World*](/posts/music/2026-07-16_cruel_world/) and I love it!

I've already loved *Scarlett* for years, but I had never really listened to the other songs from this EP until recently.  
Oh I wished I had listened to them sooner!  
I love songs in this EP so much that I even looked up the guitar chords to see if I can play them.

Now I often find myself hearing especially *Friendly Fire*, *Thursday*, and *Haunted House*.  
And I think I came to like *The Walls Are Way Too Thin* much more after I went to her live show this August.  
Her performance was great, I'm glad that I could witness it!
