Matilda Mann has been my favorite artist since I first heard her music (first I heard  “Bloom” and “The Day That I Met You”). 

Her voice, the sound of the instruments, the lyrics… everything hits the sweet spot in my heart.  

“Roxwell” is her debut album, and it's one of the best things I've got!  
I feel so happy that I own it, and the fact that I can take the CD out and play it anytime I want feels amazing. 

I love this album so much (it's not every day that you LOVE EVERY SONG in the album!) that I even ordered a poster of Roxwell on the official site (although when you live in Japan like me, you have to wait for weeks for delivery :( ).

Here's the tracklist:
1. At The End Of The Day
2. Say It Back
3. Dazed And Confused
4. Tell Me That I’m Wrong
5. See You Later
6. Worst Person Alive
7. Just Because
8. Only So Far Away
9. Meet Cute
10. Everything I’m Not
11. Common Sense
12. Autopilot
13. All That Was Said
14. Girls

Some songs always make me want to move and sing along, and other songs make me want to listen to them before I go to sleep, or during a night walk.  
I never got bored while listening from the start of the album to the end!  

There are music videos for some songs, and I think it's worth checking out!
