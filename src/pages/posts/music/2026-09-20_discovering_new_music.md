I love listening to music.  
These days I usually listen to Folk, Indie Pop and J-Pop.  

When I want to explore new songs, I often check the "Release Radar" playlist made by Spotify, but sometimes I get bored of recommended songs and I feel that I should explore unfamiliar genres as well.  

So instead, sometimes I read album reviews[^1] or ask my friends for some recommendations.

I also check:
- <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://www.last.fm/">Last.fm</a> 's recommendations

and
- <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://austinkleon.substack.com/t/mixtapes">Austin Kleon's mixtapes</a>

when I want to explore songs from the past.

---

[^1]: Reading album review on websites like <a class="icon-new-tab" target="_blank" href="https://www.rollingstone.co.uk/">Rolling Stone UK</a> helps me discover new albums that I can't find by just listening to the music picked by algorithm.
