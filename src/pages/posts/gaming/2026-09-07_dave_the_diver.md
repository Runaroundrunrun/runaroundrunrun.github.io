I bought DAVE THE DIVER this summer.

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/p85VHMpE0to?si=PBn7SqdxqJnTYEG3" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

I've finished playing all the chapters already so these days I mostly hunt for sharks (call me a shark hunter!).

As the story progressed, more features became available and I enjoyed them VERY MUCH.  
There are so many places to explore, sub-tasks to do, fish to catch...  
It's so satisfying that I feel it's worth more than 2400 yen! I don't know if I can find a game as fun as this one.

One of the things I found funny is that Dave's body shape never seemed to change.  
I mean, he dives every day and works at night so he burns a lot of calories.
And yet, he doesn't get muscular!
