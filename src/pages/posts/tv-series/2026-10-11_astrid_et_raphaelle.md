I always thought having a favorite series in my target language is one of the best ways to learn it (at least for me).

I've been learning French for a while, and I wanted to find something I would love, so that I can watch it on repeat and immerse myself in French.

A few months ago I found the perfect series for me, it's "Astrid et Raphaëlle"!

Raphaëlle, who works at a police department, and Astrid, who loves solving murder mysteries team up and solve cases together.  

What I love about this series is that the friendship of these two of course! Astrid slowly starts to adapt to the new friendship and begin to be accepted by the team, and Raphaëlle's sincere attitude on learning about autism (Astrid has autism and she faces difficulties in the process of investigating) is admirable.  
Raphaëlle is such a good friend, she supports Astrid and stays patient when Astrid is having a hard time,  and most of all, she loves Astrid for who she is. 

Astrid cares about Raphaëlle as well, and she expresses it in her own way.
It's hard to tell how she feels sometimes (even she herself doesn't know), but it's certain that Raphaëlle is one of the few people who can enter Astrid's safe space (a.k.a. paper room).  
Every little movement she makes feel adorable to me now, and sometimes funny.
I never get tired of watching her! Even when someone else's in focus, I keep looking for Astrid to see what she's doing haha.

It's not very common to find series that two female lead the story, build genuine friendship and support each other.

It's a great series.
