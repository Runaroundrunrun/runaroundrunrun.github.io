It's been months since I started writing here, but to be honest I don't know if I even like writing.  

I know I like discovering new things and places. The excitement I get from exploring those things is stronger than any other activities.  
I thought I liked singing, but I realized that I liked it only when I felt I could sing well, and not when I was practicing.  

Sure it feels better after I write down my thoughts and feelings on paper, but the act of writing itself does not feel as great as exploring or singing (it doesn't feel bad either though).

And yet I write anyway, because I hear that writing helps you think, reflect on your day, organize your feelings, deal with stress and understand the world around you better.  

Also, sometimes it's fun to read things you wrote in the past.
