Lately I check social media's feeds (especially YouTube and Instagram) first thing in the morning, and I want to stop it.

I open the apps just to see if there's anything that will make me feel good. Oftentimes, the contents I consume are not even from the accounts I follow.  
Yikes.

I consider my morning hours kind of sacred, but checking the feeds feels like destroying (or polluting) that time.

Also, it sucks that my attention is heavily affected by algorithms. I never wanted to be someone who spends a lot of time in front of the screen.

Instead of checking the feeds, maybe what I should do instead is to decide what to do in the morning. Like reading good poems or listening to [my favorite songs](/posts/music/2026-10-20_favorite_songs/), the ones that doesn't increase dopamine rapidly.

But it's easier said than done, because...you know, these services are built really well. It's difficult to fight the urge to open the apps.

Anyway, here are the things I've tried so far;
- Deleting the YouTube app[^1]
    - Didn't work. I ended up accessing YouTube from a browser...
- Hiding my electric devices
    - This one didn't work either
- Turning off watch history on YouTube
    - This seems to be working for me now. By turning it off, there will be no video in the home feed and it makes YouTube less appealing and addictive, I think.

I use Instagram less than YouTube, so I'll see if my time on Instagram will change by the actions above.
