This book is so great.  
I wish I could erase my memory and read it again!
This is the kind of book that makes reading fun... Why didn't I read it sooner? 
From beginning to end, I wanted to know what would happen next so badly that I sometimes read the end of the page first.

I've read the Japanese version. It's divided into two parts, but I only bought the first part.
I regretted that I hadn't bought the second part at the same time!

I don't think that I can write what I loved about this book without giving spoilers, so proceed only if you've read it (or seen the movie).

Have you read it?  

Are you sure you want to keep reading?  
Really?

First of all, Rocky.  
To me he was just a creepy creature at first, but as I continued reading, I grew to love him (I mean, who wouldn't?). He's kind and sincere, and he didn't give up even after his crew died (but he's not human so he might feel things a little differently than us. If he was a human he would have gone crazy or have committed suicide, right?).

Then there's Grace.  
We learn that he really didn't want to go to space knowing that it would be a one-way journey.
However, after becoming friends with Rocky, he decided to give up returning home to save Rocky and Rocky's home instead.
This sacrifice is huge, and I could tell how happy he was when he found out that he might be able to survive!

The friendship between Grace and Rocky is so genuine and precious.
They weren't scared of or vicious toward each other from the start; they built their friendship with respect and curiosity.
Not only did they overcome the language barrier, but they even risked their lives to save each other (BFF for life)!

Now I have a question; why did only Grace survive and the other crew die?
I thought that the other two crew members had the same gene as Grace, which would have allowed them to stay alive after a long coma?

I bet that if they all survived, the bond between Grace and Rocky wouldn't have been this strong.
They might not have become BFFs if they hadn't been alone!
