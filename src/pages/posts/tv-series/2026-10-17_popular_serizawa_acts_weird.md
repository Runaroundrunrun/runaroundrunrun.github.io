Recently I'm obsessed with the series *Popular Serizawa Acts Weird Around Me* .

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/II40oNJRnPA?si=M2ASw5SVBSwAsRmu" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

I watched the episode 6 everyday while it was available for free (I know I watched it too many times).

I wasn't that interested in the series at first, but I started watching clips from Instagram Live of the actors[^1] and I came to have fun while watching their interactions.  
Now I can tell how close they became through shooting the series,  and they always make me laugh so hard in every Instagram Live. 

I found out that there are many ad-lib scenes thanks to them, and now I watch the series thinking "Is this ad-lib too?", it's fun.

Normally I can't stand watching romantic scenes, it feels cheesy and makes me want to scratch my skin. But I could watch those scenes when I play the Instagram Live at the same time (it's more like I watch the whole episode to understand what they are talking about in the stream).

I began to like the series itself too, especially because:
- Serizawa's friends' ad-lib scenes are incredibly hilarious
- It's thirty-minute long. My attention span is getting short these days so I don't think I would have been able to keep focusing if it was an hour for each episodes...
- The story isn't so depressing (it can be frustrating for some people for sure, but I find it hilarious) I can watch it peacefully.[^2]
- The acting of Kousaku (who plays Suzuki) is impressive.
- I love the interactions of Saeki and his girlfriend. I hope they are happy forever 🫶

Since I don't live in Kansai area, I can't watch it on TV and I'm a bit sad about it...

The series is not over yet, but I really hope there's season 2.
