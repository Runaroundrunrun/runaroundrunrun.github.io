This May, I wrote that [I buy way more books than I can read](/posts/reading/2026-05-16_I_buy_way_more_books_than_I_can_read/).

I still buy books, but I buy less these days.

Maybe it's because I go to bookstores more often than I used to and I keep seeing the same books on the shelves, or maybe I'm more into the actual act of reading.

It's also possible that after I became unemployed, my savings are decreasing and I feel that I need to keep myself from spending too much money.  
(Or maybe I'm just tired...)

I don't think this is a bad thing, it's not like I'm out of books to read. YET.  

Despite all, bookstores are still my favourite place.
